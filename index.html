Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information · Version 1.0 · Last updated 15 August 2026

What the current app collects

Trailer To Go 1.0 is a closed test. The sections below describe the Platform in full, including features that are not switched on in this release. As the app stands today:

Name, email address, phone numberCollected
Photographs of the trailer, taken in the appCollected
Your locationNot collected
Payment or card detailsNot collected
ID number, driver’s licence, selfie or biometric dataNot collected
Files, photo library or documents on your phoneNot accessible
Microphone or audioNot accessible

The app asks the phone for five permissions only: camera, internet, notifications, start‑on‑boot and vibrate. It has no location, microphone or storage permission at all, so “the app cannot browse your photos” is enforced by the phone itself, not merely by the design. Photographs can only be taken with the in‑app camera; there is no way to choose an existing image.

Sections 2, 6 and 7 below — special personal information, payment information and location data — describe planned functionality and do not apply to this release.

Who we are

Trailer To Go ("we", "us") is the responsible party for the personal information processed through the Platform. Contact our Information Officer at privacy@trailertogo.co.za. Trailer To Go is a marketplace that connects trailer Owners, Customers and Drivers; we process only the information needed to run that marketplace.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — name, surname, username, email, phone number(s), home/business address.
  • Owner details — business name, contact numbers, operating hours, area of operation, payout details, trailer listing information.
  • Customer & Driver verification — ID number, driver's licence details (code, validity, restrictions, issuer), vehicle make/model/year, VIN, licence plate and disc, and related photos.
  • Booking & usage data — bookings, messages, ratings, reviews, support requests, and device/log information.

2. Special personal information

To verify identity and eligibility to tow, we process special personal information: your ID number, driver's licence data read from the licence barcode, and a selfie/photo used for identity matching (biometric verification). We process this only for verification, fraud prevention and safety, with your consent, and we apply stricter access controls to it.

3. Why we process it

  • To create and manage your account and verify Owners, Customers and Drivers.
  • To publish listings, enable search, process Bookings and facilitate payments.
  • To match delivery/collection jobs to approved Drivers.
  • To share only the details needed to complete a rental between the relevant parties.
  • To prevent fraud, keep the Platform safe, resolve disputes and comply with law.
  • To provide support and, where you agree, send service and marketing messages.

4. Lawful basis & consent

We process personal information where it is necessary to provide the Platform and perform our agreement with you, to comply with a legal obligation, to pursue our legitimate interests (such as safety and fraud prevention) in a balanced way, or with your consent — for example for special personal information and for marketing. You may withdraw consent at any time, though some processing is necessary to use the Platform.

5. What we share, and with whom

We share only what is necessary to complete a rental. When a Booking is confirmed, the relevant Owner, Customer and Driver see the contact and pick-up/delivery details needed to carry out that Booking — nothing more. We do not sell your personal information.

We may also share information with: payment providers (to process transactions); verification and fraud-screening providers; IT, hosting and support providers acting as our operators under written agreements; and law-enforcement, regulators or courts where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Payment information

Payments are handled through a payment provider. Card details you enter are used to process the transaction and are not stored in full by us; we retain only limited information such as the last four digits and the transaction record.

7. Location data

We use approximate location to power search, maps and depot-finding. An Owner's exact pick-up address is not shown publicly and is shared with a Customer or Driver only once a Booking is confirmed. You can control device location permissions in your browser or device settings.

8. Security

We take reasonable, appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, unauthorised access and misuse, including access controls on verification documents. No system is completely secure; if a data breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

9. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal, tax, accounting and dispute-resolution requirements (for example, retaining booking and payment records for the period required by law). When no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

10. Cross-border processing

Some service providers (such as hosting or analytics) may process information outside South Africa. Where this happens, we take steps required by POPIA to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection.

11. Your rights under POPIA

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive or out of date.
  • Object to processing, and withdraw consent, in certain circumstances.
  • Ask that we stop using your information for direct marketing.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

Some information must be retained to meet legal obligations or to complete a transaction, so deletion may be limited accordingly. To exercise a right, contact our Information Officer.

12. Cookies & analytics

We use essential cookies/local storage to keep you signed in and to operate the Platform, and may use analytics to understand and improve it. You can control non-essential cookies through your browser settings.

13. Children

The Platform is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

14. Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your personal information, you may contact our Information Officer, or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg@justice.gov.za.

15. Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version is always available on the Platform. For any privacy query, contact our Information Officer at privacy@trailertogo.co.za.
